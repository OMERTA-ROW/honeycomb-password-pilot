# Honeycomb Family Guardian — private preview

## Working UI / simulated capabilities
Optional, off-by-default `/family-guardian`, reachable from Vault navigation. Local fictional profile creation uses fixed bee aliases and age bands only; no free-form child-data entry. Any preview visitor can explore these samples: this is not a real guardian account or authorization screen. Nothing is saved to accounts, browser storage or a server. Leaving/refreshing clears the state; turning off clears profiles and sample review actions.

Guardian/child acknowledgments gate simulated invitation and child acceptance. They are neither verified guardianship nor legally sufficient consent. Withdrawal unlinks the sample; revocation stops sample safeguards. Blocking defaults to parent review. Automatic mode simulates blocking only supported high-confidence link threats; uncertain phishing/contact-pattern signals and unsupported purchase controls always require review. Contact-pattern alerts do not establish grooming or accuse any person. Age bands provide discussion suggestions, not validated policy or legal thresholds.

Safety review contains four fixed fictional scenarios; child warnings are non-blaming and encourage trusted-adult help. Notification preview contains no child/sender identity or message body and sends nothing. Evidence dialog downloads only a fixed fictional, unsigned summary separating observation, interpretation, missing records and absence of corroboration. No real message, attachment, image, URL, identity or payment data is collected, reported or preserved. Do not redistribute sensitive imagery; future reports require deliberate recipient/data review and appropriate official reporting paths. No automatic external reporting, links followed, model prompts or mail.

## Real capabilities: unbuilt / blocked
- Family profiles, verified guardian authority, age/jurisdiction-specific consent, transparent device permissions and child participation/help: not built for real users.
- Family-scoped RLS and server authorization, role restrictions, invitations with expiry/single use/revoke, consent withdrawal, abuse/coercion safeguards and isolated-family tests: required before real data. Existing Vault account login is not family authorization.
- On-device encryption, ciphertext evidence storage, key distribution/recovery/removal, retention/deletion and independent security/child-safety/privacy review: required, not implemented. No custom crypto or invented encryption guarantees.
- Native/device enrollment, provider OAuth, classifiers, action readback, notifications and real protection health: no integrations exist. Never show active protection from a preference or sample link.
- Minimum collection by design: prefer local risk categories, coarse timestamps and generic notifications rather than message bodies. No raw message content in logs/analytics/models. No sale or transfer of children's personal messages/data to JAX or data brokers; no covert surveillance or location monitoring.
- Every capability needs narrowly scoped permission, supported API, consent, freshness/health, reversible action where feasible and per-device/channel revocation. Vault secrets remain excluded, including from any future Omni executor.

## Platform feasibility gates / official references
These are prerequisites, not evidence of project entitlement or functionality:
- Apple Family Controls / Managed Settings / Device Activity require supported native implementation, explicit authorization and distribution entitlement approval. They do not grant access to all messages or third-party chat contents: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols and https://developer.apple.com/documentation/managedsettings .
- Apple IdentityLookup filtering is limited to supported unknown-sender SMS/MMS, not universal iMessage/chat access: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/identitylookup/sms-and-mms-message-filtering . iPadOS/device availability requires device validation.
- Android default SMS roles, runtime permissions and Google Play restricted-permission policies must be satisfied: https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/permissions/default-handlers and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10208820 . No private third-party chat inspection or universal remote control assumed.
- Email requires per-user authorized narrow provider scopes and assessment where required; browser link protection requires a reviewed extension/provider. Neither is connected here.
- Purchases remain under official Apple Ask to Buy / Google Play parental purchase approvals or issuer systems: https://support.apple.com/105055 and https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7039872 . These do not provide Vault a universal interception/reversal API. No cards, PINs, payment tokens or biometrics collected.

## Acceptance before launch
Test unrelated-family denial; guardian/member boundaries; authenticated invitation expiry/reuse/revocation; verified consent and withdrawal; device permissions/revocation; correct supported confidence thresholds; false-positive recovery; child transparency/help; minimized notifications/evidence; encrypted persistence and key deletion; no content in logs/models; real offline/stale/unsupported failures. Independent review must address guardian misuse, coercion, changing family relationships, age/jurisdiction consent and reporting obligations. Private preview only: no launch, publication, paid provisioning or real child fixtures.
